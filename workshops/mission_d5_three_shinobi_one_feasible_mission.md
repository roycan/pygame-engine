## 🥷 PHASE 1 — The MoSCoW Strike (5 min — Scope Master leads, all decide)

1. Open the **feature menu** (`mission_d5_feature_menu.md`). Each feature is
   pre-priced in **effort tokens** — relative work estimates, not hours.
2. Mark every feature **M / S / C / W**. Start from the value center: feature 1
   (chase rules, 2 tokens) is **Must** — without it there is no predator–prey dynamic.
3. Sum your **M + S** tokens. The rule: **M + S ≤ 12.** If you are over, move features
   to **C** or **W** until you fit.
4. The Quartermaster checks feasibility: can three Grade-9 shinobi deliver this in
   three milestones? Cut deeper if not.

**Predict, before you total:** which TWO features did everyone want to keep — and which
one did your squad drop first? ________________________________

**✅ Check:** M + S total = ____ tokens (≤ 12) · exclusions so far: ____ features

## 🥷 PHASE 3 — The Milestone Relay (4 min — Coordinator leads)

**Four words, precisely defined:**
- **Task** — one piece of work ("draw the grid", "write the chase rule").
- **Milestone** — a dated checkpoint bundling related tasks, ending in a delivery.
- **Deliverable** — the concrete thing the milestone hands over.
- **Team lead** — coordinates the milestone and is **accountable** for its deliverable.
  The lead does **not** do all the work — the squad does.

Fill the **§D Milestone Relay** on your card — three milestones, a **different lead per
milestone**, each with a deliverable and a "done when". Example directions (adjust to
your chosen scope):

1. **Walking Wild** — the predator–prey chase works on the grid. *Done when:* a chase
   can be demonstrated turn by turn.
2. **The Living Grove** — one selected mechanic that makes populations move. *Done
   when:* population changes over time can be shown.
3. **The Showcase** — finished demonstration and evidence; **population charts only if
   you selected them**. *Done when:* the Biology Department can read the story of the
   board.

**⚖ Honest scale:** these three milestones are a **preliminary simulation for this
exercise**. Your real SG 16 project plan must include **at least three milestones per
quarter, and every member leads at least one milestone in every quarter** — squads of
four propose a fourth milestone or a double lead, with the sensei's approval.

## 🥷 PHASE 4 — Skills, Risks & the Pitch (4 min — individual lines, in parallel)

1. **Self-regulated learning — one line per shinobi, on the card §E:** a skill the
   project needs that you personally must acquire (e.g., "population charts"),
   **how** you will learn it, **what resource or strategy** you will use, and the
   **evidence** that you learned it ("one working chart from my own data").
   *Candidate libraries may be named — SG 17 will teach frameworks and libraries in
   depth. Naming now is not committing.*
2. **One project risk + mitigation** on the card: what could sink Project Safari, and
   what the squad does about it early. (Example: "breeding numbers might explode and
   crash the board — test limits in milestone 1.")
3. **Pitch prep (30 seconds):** one sentence, one speaker —
   *"We will deliver ______ first because ______; we are excluding ______ because ______."*

## ⏱ VERSION C — extended (25–30 min)

Version B **plus**:
- **+3 min · The Gamekeeper Changes the Deal (the Triangle gets teeth):** mid-mission,
  the client requests **one more 3-token feature** ("add food patches — the Biology
  Department insists") **without moving the deadline.** Twelve is already spent. The
  squad must choose, in writing: **cut 3 tokens of scope** (which, and why it is the
  right cut) · **or justify more time/resources** (what changes, who approves) ·
  **or postpone the feature** to a documented later milestone. No silent absorbing.
- **+5 min · Own-project transfer:** redo the card for the squad's **own** project idea
  (SG 16 Activity 1 begins here — your feature list, your estimates, your relay).
- **+2 min · Peer feasibility check:** partner squad reviews your card with three
  questions: *M+S ≤ 12? · does every requirement appear in a milestone? · would a
  stranger know what to build first?*

**Realistic total: 28–30 min. If time collapses, the client-change negotiation goes
first — it is the strongest 3 minutes in the deck of this mission.**
