## SG 16 accuracy flags (source untouched; deck/scroll teach corrected versions)

1. **Triangle ≠ success guarantee:** SG 16 defines success as deadline + budget + scope
   met. True as far as it goes — but the triangle trades off, and a project can satisfy
   all three corners and still be useless. P-1 puts **quality/value at the center**.
2. **"Two common methodologies"** (Waterfall, Agile) undersells SG 15's nine models and
   carries a typo (*"SLDC"*). Taught relationship: PM methodologies organize the SDLC
   phases; Waterfall/Agile are the two most-cited PM approaches; the nine-model menu
   stands.
3. **Milestone rotation:** "at least three milestones per quarter, each member leads
   one" — maps perfectly to squads of three; fours need a fourth milestone or a
   documented double lead. P-1's relay makes the rotation concrete and defines "lead" =
   coordinates + accountable, not solo labor.
4. **SRL never operationalized:** the Annex B proposal template has **no field** for
   skills-to-learn or learning strategies. P-1's §E supplies the practice.
   **Recommendation:** teacher-approved supplementary fields on the Annex B draft
   (or a separately collected SRL line) — the official template itself is not modified.
5. **Cross-disciplinary field missing from Annex B:** integration lives only in Annex A
   and the objectives. **Recommendation:** one "Integration:" line in the proposal
   description (teacher-approved supplement) — the card's §A rehearses it.
6. **20-pt rubric placement:** the text says "found at the end of Annex C," but it is
   printed between Annex B and Annex C. Cosmetic; flagged.
7. **Oral presentation realism:** 45–50 minutes for ~33 squads with per-member delivery
   ≈ 80 seconds per squad. **Teacher decision required** — parallel stations,
   representative pitches, or gallery walk (prep §G). Not assumed approved.
8. **Proposal → plan relationship:** "a version of the following" is ambiguous — students
   may think they resubmit the SG 15 Cookie documents. Clarified: the SG 15 annex
   **formats** are templates for the **approved project's own** documentation. Due
   "middle of the quarter" — calendar it.
